About Us

RockAuto Savings Guide is an independent, reader-supported resource that tracks and manually verifies RockAuto discount codes. We are not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by RockAuto.

Our process is simple: we review shopper reports (primarily from Reddit and similar public forums), check whether a code actually applied at checkout, and label each one based on the evidence we found. We do not scrape or auto-generate coupon listings, and we do not guarantee that any code will work by the time you read this page.

The goal of this site is to save readers time and money by separating codes that are actually confirmed to work from ones that are outdated, unverified, or simply copied from other coupon sites without checking.
