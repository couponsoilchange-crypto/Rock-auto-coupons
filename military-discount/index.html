Does RockAuto Have a Military Discount Code?

No. We found no current RockAuto military discount advertised as of this check. What it does have are reusable 5% codes, open to every shopper, military or not, entered in the "How Did You Hear About Us?" field at checkout. If you've been specifically hunting for a "military" version of that code, stop: it doesn't exist, and typing extra words like "military" into that field will only break the code, since it only accepts a plain number.
